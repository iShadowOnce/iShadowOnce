## About Me :

I'm a Computer Engineering student and a developer specialized in Minecraft servers. During my free time, I dedicate my efforts to my personal project called SOC Labs. This initiative focuses primarily on plugin development and advanced configuration of my own Minecraft server. Additionally, the project explores other areas of development, such as the creation of scripts for the FiveM platform using the LUA programming language. In general terms, SOC Labs is a comprehensive development environment where I materialize several creations, although its main focus lies in the previously mentioned areas.

- I am currently improving in the languages I already know.
- My native language is Spanish but I speak a little bit of English.
- In my free time apart from working at SOC Labs, I like to play video games and make streams for fun.
